- Quote and cite any figure in research, journalism, reports, and presentations.

- Do not imply the AI Layoffs Index endorses your use, and do not present derived
  or altered figures as the official reading.

Why not a Creative Commons license
----------------------------------
The index is a derived work built from many sources that carry their own,
different licenses: some are US government public domain (FRED, Census), some are
open government or Creative Commons, and some are reproduced from company filings
and press under their own terms. A single blanket Creative Commons grant over the
whole would claim rights the project does not hold over those underlying materials.
So the project uses a plain attribution license over its own work (the index, the
scoring, the compiled register), and asks you to credit the source for any
specific underlying figure. That is the honest boundary, and it is why this deposit
declares custom terms of use rather than a CC waiver.

No warranty
-----------
The AI Layoffs Index is a research observatory. Every figure traces to a public
source and is provided as-is, for information, without warranty of any kind. It
measures AI's estimated impact on jobs relative to AI's own history since 2023, not
total unemployment, and it is explicit about its uncertainty. Decisions you make
from it are your own.

Licensing for organizations
---------------------------
The open license above covers the vast majority of uses at no cost. Organizations
that build on the data can license it under written terms, with custom extracts of
the register and a note of what changed at each update. See
https://ailayoffs.org/pricing.
